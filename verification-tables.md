# Verification tables

Reference tables for the universal-research skill. SKILL.md says when to read each one.

## Claim-type verification matrix

Used in Phase 7. Classify the claim, apply the preferred verification, and check for the typical failure.

| Claim type | Preferred verification | Typical failure to check |
|---|---|---|
| Numerical or statistical | Trace to the original dataset or report; recompute; check units, denominators, period, sample size, uncertainty, definitions | Unit or scale errors, relative vs absolute change, cherry-picked period, misquoted figure |
| Software, hardware, or protocol behavior | Documentation, specification, changelog, or source code for the exact version; when tools allow, a minimal test with the actual output reported | Version mismatch, deprecated or removed behavior, documentation that differs from real behavior |
| Performance or benchmark | Method, hardware, workload, version, and who ran it; independent or reproduced results | Vendor-run tests, unrepresentative workloads, missing baselines |
| Scientific or medical | Original study or systematic review; design, sample, effect size, uncertainty, funding, replication, retraction status | Preprint treated as settled, association presented as causation, animal or cell results generalized to people |
| Historical | Contemporary or primary documents; separate what sources report from later interpretation | Later works repeating one early account, anachronism, partisan framing |
| Legal, regulatory, or policy | Current official text for the correct jurisdiction and date, including amendments | Outdated or wrong-jurisdiction text, a summary mistaken for the law |
| Current status (prices, availability, latest versions, office holders) | Live official source, with the retrieval date | Stale or cached pages, memory |
| Causal claims | Study design (controlled or randomized over observational), confounders, mechanism, replication | Correlation presented as causation |
| Forecasts, opinions, estimates | Identify author, method, and track record; present as such | Forecasts reported as facts |

## Corroboration floor by criticality

Used in Phases 8 and 9. Criticality (Low, Medium, High) is rated in Phase 2.

| Criticality | Minimum for Verified |
|---|---|
| Low | One directly inspected source of tier 1 to 3, with no known contradiction. |
| Medium | One directly inspected tier 1 or 2 source, plus one independent corroboration (different origin or successful independent test), plus a falsification search. |
| High | Two independent origins at tier 1 or 2, or one tier 1 source plus an independent test or reproduction; falsification search; currency and scope confirmed. For health, legal, financial, or safety decisions, also state that the research does not replace a qualified professional. |
